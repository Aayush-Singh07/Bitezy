⚡ BITEZY DELIVERY PRICING MODEL (FINAL)


---

🟢 RIDER COST (FIXED)

👉 ₹15 per delivery (flat payout)

predictable cost

easy scaling

no complexity



---

🟡 CUSTOMER DELIVERY (DYNAMIC)


---

🔻 Tier 1: Orders < ₹49

Customer pays: ₹25 delivery

Bitezy pays rider: ₹15


👉 Net: +₹10 (covers ops + losses elsewhere)


---

🟠 Tier 2: ₹49 – ₹89

Customer pays: ₹15 delivery

Bitezy pays rider: ₹15


👉 Net: ₹0 (break-even delivery)


---

🟢 Tier 3: ₹89+ (Hero Tier 🚀)

Customer pays: ₹0 delivery

Bitezy pays rider: ₹15


👉 Net: -₹15 (subsidized by food margin)


---

🎯 CORE STRATEGY LINE

> “Bitezy uses a fixed rider payout model of ₹15 per delivery, while dynamically charging customers between ₹25 and ₹0 based on cart value. Higher-value orders subsidize delivery costs, enabling free delivery above ₹89 while maintaining overall unit profitability.”




---

🧠 WHY THIS WORKS (IMPORTANT)


---

👉 Small orders → profit (₹25 collected)
👉 Mid orders → neutral
👉 Large orders → loss on delivery BUT profit on food

➡️ Overall system = profitable


---

💣 REAL GAME HERE


---

You’re doing:

> Delivery margin + food margin = total profit



Not just delivery.
